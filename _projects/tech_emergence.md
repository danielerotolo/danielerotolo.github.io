### Aim
The **Indicators of Technological Emergence** project develops and validates quantitative indicators to identify and track technologies as they emerge — moving from early-stage scientific research toward market application. The project builds on bibliometric and patent-based methods to characterise the growth, novelty, and coherence that typically signal an emerging technology, providing tools that can support technology forecasting and innovation policy.
