### Aim
The **Exploring the Interdependencies of Research Funders in the UK** project examines how public, charitable, and private funders of biomedical and cancer research in the UK relate to and depend on one another. Rather than treating funders as independent actors, the project maps the network of relationships — co-funding, complementary remits, and shared beneficiary organisations — that shape how research funding decisions are made, with a focus on the implications for cancer research funding strategy.
