### Aim
The **CANCERSCREEN** (Screening for Cancer in the Post-Genomic Era) project examines how advances in genomic science and technology are reshaping cancer screening. As post-genomic technologies — including genetic and molecular tests — are increasingly incorporated into screening programmes, the project investigates the scientific, clinical, regulatory, and policy challenges this raises, and how these challenges are being addressed by the actors and institutions responsible for developing and governing cancer screening.
