### Aim
The **PACT** (Learning from Past And Current Transitions: A new PACT for fair and resilient European regions) aims to analyse the social and economic consequences of the so-called ‘twin transition’ towards digitalisation and green transformation in European regions; regions already shaped by earlier socio-economic transformations, notably moves from manufacturing to service economies. The project mixes quantitative and qualitative methods in a combined historical and contemporary study that will generate knowledge and insight for a more just twin transition.
