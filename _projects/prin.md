### Aim
This project investigates the intra- and inter-organizational factors that influence the success of sustainability-oriented firms, with a focus on Italian SMEs and comparisons between startups and mature firms. Internally, the study examines how managerial team characteristics—particularly multidisciplinarity and gender diversity—and business process orientations affect firm performance. Externally, it analyzes the role of business ecosystems, including the types and reputation of ecosystem actors, relationship structures, firm centrality, and orchestration capabilities. Firm performance will be assessed across the three sustainability dimensions: economic, environmental, and social. The project adopts quantitative research methods to empirically test theoretical frameworks using both primary and secondary data. Traditional indicators, such as financial and intellectual property data, will be integrated with innovative text-mining approaches applied to firms’ websites, annual and sustainability reports. The findings are expected to provide theoretical and managerial insights while supporting the transition toward a more sustainable economy and society.
