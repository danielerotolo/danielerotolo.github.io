### Aim
The **Barriers to Interdisciplinary Research** project investigates the structural, institutional, and cultural barriers that constrain interdisciplinary research within UK higher education. Interdisciplinary work is widely recognised as important for tackling complex scientific and societal challenges, yet researchers pursuing it often face obstacles related to funding structures, career progression, publication norms, and departmental organisation. The project identifies these barriers and considers how funders and institutions might address them to better support interdisciplinary research.
