### Aim
The **Deep Transition** project examines how history can inform understanding of the fundamental, system-wide transformation of the socio-technical systems (energy, mobility, food, communication) that emerged from the first Industrial Revolution — collectively termed the "First Deep Transition." Drawing on insights from history and transitions research, the project investigates the extent to which the norms, values, and rules underpinning this First Deep Transition need to be renegotiated to catalyse a "Second Deep Transition" toward sustainability. The project combines historical case studies with conceptual and empirical work to explore how deliberate, directed transformation of interlinked systems can be achieved.
